About Chinakkathoor Pooram

Chinakkathoor Pooram is the annual festival of Sree Chinakkathoor Bhagavathi Temple, celebrated with elephants, traditional percussion ensembles, and processions organized by the Thekkumangalam Desam Chinakkathoor Pooram Committee. Devotees and well-wishers are invited to contribute towards the festival through the donation categories below.
